What We Do

Every roof, done once.
Done right.

From full replacements to emergency storm response — premium materials, manufacturer-certified installation, and a job site left cleaner than we found it.

Roof Replacement & Repair

Full tear-offs, re-roofs, and repairs with GAF & TAMKO architectural shingles — installed to manufacturer spec so your warranty actually holds. Leaks, missing shingles, and flashing failures diagnosed honestly.

Solar Ventilation

Attic Breeze-certified solar-powered attic fans that lower cooling costs by up to 30%, eliminate humidity build-up, and extend the lifespan of your roof deck — 100% solar powered.

Storm Damage Restoration

Severe Texas hail and wind damage assessment with forensic photo reports. We provide clear, undeniable proof so you have the exact documentation your carrier requires.

Inspections & Maintenance

Free multi-point roof inspections with a comprehensive written report, aerial photos, and honest recommendations — know the exact condition of your roof before problems compound.

Insurance Documentation Support

Our experienced team works alongside insurance adjusters, itemizing every damaged component from ridge cap to drip edge so your claim is handled fairly and completely.

Commercial Roofing

Flat and low-slope commercial systems — TPO, modified bitumen, silicone restoration coatings, and metal roofing with zero disruption to your daily business operations.
